## What this increment closes

On preproduction x64-review, one explicitly issued request can reach kind-11 intake once. Two independent processes and a fresh process after restart must reach the same disposition from durable evidence. Drive the production storage and recovery procedures with the local fixture path. Stop at confirmed proposal intake (task/control 1/0). The provider call, project settings, operator-workflow integration, approval, permit and worker dispatch belong to later scopes.

## Catalog identity and evidence of issuance

**Use a single reviewed, immutable catalog revision.** Reject duplicate identities, identical canonical payloads under different identities, and duplicate SHA-256 digests during catalog construction. Compare full canonical bytes as well as the digest at validation. Pin the revision at issuance and retain it for recovery. Reassigning an identity, changing bytes or introducing aliases requires catalog review and a migration plan for outstanding and historical requests. Treat the display label as audit context. It grants no distinct authority over identical bytes.

| Choice | Benefit | Cost or limit |
|---|---|---|
| One identity per payload | A confirmed proposal's complete payload selects one entry in the pinned catalog. Handles the identical-bytes substitution case at construction. | Forbids semantic aliases. Before any proposal exists, a rewritten issuance can still switch A to B along with its digest. |
| Permit identical bytes under distinct identities | Preserves intent distinctions for operators and audit. | Payload and F can never identify the issued alias. Every admission and historical check needs independent evidence of the exact identity. |
| Trust only a live issuer's in-memory copy before intake | Smallest live check. Killing the issuer extinguishes its in-memory eligibility. | A new process cannot independently verify historical issuance or expiry. Deleting an unused issuance or rolling back expiry can leave SQLite internally consistent. This option alone cannot close the durable integrity obligation. |
| Bind issuance and expiry to existing custody-confirmed event history | Detects changing or removing a request before kind 11 exists. Reuses the existing independent checkpoint and recovery protocol. | Requires reviewed event encodings, producer rules, reader compatibility, bounded replay, two additional fault boundaries and their proofs. |

**Owner decision.** Combine catalog uniqueness with custody-covered issuance and expiry. For example, changing both the issued identity and its digest to another valid catalog entry passes a uniqueness check. Compare them with the confirmed issuance before creating an attempt. Reverting an expiry must also fail against the confirmed history. A signed issuance alone cannot establish freshness after expiry. A separate independently protected issuance ledger could carry both facts, at the cost of another durability protocol and reconciliation across stores. No additional witness of *consumption* replaces the SQLite pair selected in ADR-0011.

## Proposed operations and ownership

1. **Provision.** Quiesce the enrolled store and explicitly install and read back the exact auxiliary schema. Enforce unique issued IDs in the store and unique consumption by ID and by full event Ref. Bind rows to store and epoch. Refuse conflicting schemas, duplicate IDs and inconsistent existing rows. Normal opens and historical replay perform validation only. Use the existing single-task, single-attempt envelope.
2. **Issue.** Under the writer gate, generate 16 random bytes and encode 32 lowercase hex characters. Bind the store, epoch, request ID, catalog revision, identity, digest and expiry policy in a runtime-produced issuance event. Write its local issuance row and event atomically, read them back, and obtain custody Confirm before returning eligibility for a future call. Bind eligibility to the current runtime session and boot. End it on cancellation, deadline equality or loss of that session. Before readiness after restart, serialize recovery and expire outstanding requests from the lost session. A concurrent caller opening the store does not by itself constitute recovery. The deadline for this fixture profile is 30 elapsed seconds from issuance, shortened by the enclosing deadline. Start a fresh request after expiry. Persist a typed, custody-covered expiry disposition exactly once. An ambiguous result withholds eligibility.
3. **Admit.** Reacquire the writer gate and verify history, the request schema and inventory, deadline, original intent and catalog selection. Require an outstanding issuance and the increment 1 result. Reject unknown, stale, consumed or mismatched IDs before directory creation, task insertion or proposal Prepare. Check fresh BOOTTIME under the writer gate. Successful consumption requires proof that COMMIT completed strictly before expiry. A pre-COMMIT sample alone cannot prove that condition when WAL sync stalls. Under the writer gate, sample BOOTTIME again after COMMIT returns. If strictly-before-expiry completion cannot be proven, keep the pair spent, withhold admission and Confirm, and require reconciliation. Recovery may not infer timely completion from a stored pre-commit observation. Include a commit spanning expiry and a crash between COMMIT and the next sample in the 2c proof. Intake uses the catalog bytes. Preserve the existing order of directory creation, TaskCreated, proposal Prepare, SQLite commit, exact read-back and Confirm. Inside the existing kind-11 `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`, insert both event and insert-only consumption or roll back both. The second insertion failure must roll back the first. Keep the gate through read-back and Confirm. Never hold it while awaiting provider I/O.
4. **Mark provenance.** Before proposal Prepare, durably mark the newly created original attempt directory with its consumed-or-pending request ID. Set a versioned directory xattr create-only through the held descriptor, fsync that directory successfully, then permit proposal Prepare and the pair COMMIT. This preserves the directory file inventory qualified in Phase 3. Preserve the parent-directory sync and original device/inode checks. This leaves the marker filename and occupied-leaf oracle untouched. Qualify xattr support, permissions, sync, replacement refusal and retention on x64-review. Missing support or failed sync withholds progress. An xattr is independent of a SQLite-only rewrite under the recorded trust assumptions. A runtime UID that controls the directory can modify its attributes. Claims against simultaneous directory and database compromise require a stronger witness.
5. **Recover.** Before admitting new work, compare confirmed issuance and expiry history, request rows, confirmed kind 11 (or the exact pending pledge), and the directory inventory in both directions. Verify original directory identity, full Ref, event digest, F, payload and catalog identity. An exact pending pair may finish historical Confirm after these checks. Expire stale outstanding IDs only when no conflicting evidence exists. Record the reason or report unavailable audit persistence. Preserve evidence and holds on mismatch. Recovery cannot create a proposal, recover a live issuance, void consumption, remove provenance or free an ID for reuse.

Historical local kind-11 events still use their existing wire bytes and require no issuance. A missing consumption and missing provenance must never cause a previously model-originated proposal to be silently classified as historical local. Enumerate the original directories independently of the SQLite consumption query. Within the SQLite-only rewrite case, the retained mark still identifies the model path. If the original directory is lost or its identity cannot be established, preserve the existing hold. Existing approval, receipt and finalize entry points that can confirm a pending proposal must use the same binding check or withhold progress. Readers that encounter an unknown request event must fail closed. Audit-only request facts leave task/control versions unchanged.

## Acceptance on the real boundary

For each run retain before/after custody checkpoint and pending pledge, request rows, exact proposal bytes and count, task/control projection, directory identity and provenance, typed result and audit disposition. Count intake effects separately from successful admissions. Record whether a losing process saw a busy gate and repeat after release to establish the durable verdict. The positive control proves one confirmed pair at 1/0. Replay or restart adds zero admissions.

| Fixture group | Required observations |
|---|---|
| Catalog and early binding | Reject two identities with identical bytes, including empty payloads. With an outstanding A request, substitute valid B and its matching digest in SQLite before intake. Also exercise unknown, expired, mismatched and duplicate replies. A normal ineligible reply has zero directory, task and proposal-pledge effects. A tampered history holds for reconciliation. |
| Six discrepancy fixtures | Independently run **deleted committed row**, **altered binding**, **retargeted full Ref**, **provenance without a row**, **row without matching provenance**, and **SQLite-only rollback**. Each gives reconciliation and zero new admissions. Alter each field independently, including IDs and F. Use a second genuinely valid event for retargeting, including one from a separately enrolled store if required by the one-task limit. |
| Race and stale delivery | Release competing processes against one issued ID. Show at most one pair and one task/directory creation. Repeat after issuer death, fresh startup and lost acknowledgment. Old IDs cannot be reissued or admitted. |
| Issuance and expiry | Interrupt before and after each transaction and before and after Confirm. Withhold egress until confirmed. Recover an exact pledged fact once. Remove or revert issuance or expiry without changing proposal history. Detect disagreement with custody. Exercise deadline equality, cancellation and reboot without trusting stale elapsed-clock values. |
| Intake and recovery | Stop after directory creation, provenance write and sync, TaskCreated, proposal Prepare, each insert, WAL commit, before Confirm and after Confirm before reply. Inject write/sync/commit faults. Grade surviving bytes. Rollback leaves neither member of the pair. Earlier effects may survive. Preserve a missing-event pledge, one-sided provenance and task-only holds. Exact committed pairs confirm once. |
| Compatibility and resource limits | Run direct Phase 3 intake, legacy pending recovery, approval and finalization regressions. Try an older reader against new events. Reject unavailable schema, bad ownership, missing catalog revision and exhausted time/visit/storage budgets. Verify bounded scanning and durable audit under the measured envelope. |

Documentation and governance checks qualify this scope text only. Increment 2 closes on matching-kernel evidence for all rows, independent read-back, and an updated traceability record. Implementation is limited to the four reviewed work packages. Adapter egress and runtime activation remain separate decisions.
