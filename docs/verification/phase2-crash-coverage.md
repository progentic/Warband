## Cold recovery feasibility

On x64-review (kernel `6.8.0-142-generic`, source `8a53900`), three fresh stores each held one GENESIS, 33 REJECTION receipts and 33 PENDING/COMMITTED pairs. The unit is one custody frame: 100 frames, 33 canonical SQLite events. All imports were generated by the custody and importer paths. Independent checks walked the ordinal/hash chain, linked every event to the authenticated checkpoint and matched each receipt digest, full binding and watermark. The resulting projection has zero tasks. Normal replay, the same-snapshot live reader and the independent oracle agreed. This case measures one event-bearing receipt history; the mixed-task functional proof remains separate.

Generation including fixture setup took 2.147, 2.110 and 1.728 s. Each normal cold start reached authenticated Verify; a measurement-only build on the same bytes timed recover_checkpoint at 18.597, 18.975 and 17.778 ms against the 60 s deadline. Start of the measured service invocation through the first authenticated reply took 105.650, 109.114 and 105.057 ms including harness overhead. Guest page cache was uncontrolled. Cold startup examined exactly 100 frames and has no production visit-debit counter. The direct cursor-zero Verify examined 102 records in custody (100 validations, one selection, one embedded-frame check). Its raw fixture client has no session budget.

At 100 frames, clean reconciliation used 4,225 actual examinations / 6,867 charged visits and replay used 4,324 / 6,933, identical in every repetition. Exchange debits were 6,834 in each. Replay counted extraction, prefix validation, reduction, receipt reads and custody scans at their examination sites. Receipt-only live comparison was run separately from the metered replay. First-run compilation exposed an unused measurement import, corrected before the reported runs. The macOS copy of the five raw JSONL samples matched the guest SHA-256 `71c98ebbe7f1724f63a020c52d566bb15b79896dcaa150bb643991b4fb39f004`.

**10,000-frame preflight.** For this generator, n denials give T=1+3n frames and n events. The Slice 4 import formula is (25n²+33n)/2+3 debits; clean reconciliation costs (n+1)(2T+1)+n and full replay costs (n+1)(2T+1)+3n. A single 151-frame pilot (50 imports) reproduced 32,078 / 15,503 / 15,603 debits, with 151 cold examinations in 20.281 ms. The exact 10,000 mix (3,333 receipts/imports) would take 138,916,110 import and 66,693,333 replay debits. The ordinary 80,000 budget fails preflight even if generation is split into smaller passes. No 10,000-frame generation or deadline margin is claimed. At tip 10,000 even three receipts require four Verify calls and 80,004 debits before reading SQLite. A clean single-epoch mix with one GENESIS, one receipt and 4,999 confirmed events would meet the frame arithmetic and cost 54,999 replay debits, but the isolated live path admits one task/run and has no qualified generator for that event sequence. Proto accepted the measured 100-frame case and pilot-validated projection as Phase 2 evidence for this receipt workload on 2026-11-22.
