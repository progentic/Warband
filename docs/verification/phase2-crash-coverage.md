# Phase 2 durable-boundary coverage

Inventory at `2f12efc`. Checklist numbers come from the [verification plan](phase2-verification-plan.md#durable-boundary-crash-checklist). The [ADR-0005 recovery table](../decisions/0005-event-integrity-acceptance-and-recovery.md#crash-and-integrity-recovery-table) determines the disposition from the surviving bytes. ## Evidence key

- **Prepare kills:** On x64-review (kernel `6.8.0-142-generic`), the receipt-import harness captured the enrolled tip-2 history and head, WAL-mode database bytes and empty tables before each kill. For the torn case it reconstructed the ordinal-3 PENDING header from the previous record hash and computed its header hash. The `prepare_kill` entries report the caller outcome, suffix length/digest and replay result. The custodian-kill cases also retain the observed suffix in hex.
- **Import kill:** x64-review and hosted Ubuntu 24.04, PR #57 head `7c30d9d`. The parent observed the exact child stopped after SQLite COMMIT, killed and reaped it, and restarted the normal importer twice. [Harness](https://github.com/progentic/Warband/blob/7c30d9d/tests/integration/linux/test_receipt_import.py) · [Ubuntu run](https://github.com/progentic/Warband/actions/runs/44857822010/job/136704034490)
- **Point 4 replay:** On x64-review (kernel `6.8.0-142-generic`), the receipt-import harness ran production `replay-noop` as the runtime UID against the killed importer's database and custodian. The runtime binaries were built from `fed900d`. The three `post_commit_replay` entries in the harness log give the stage, authenticated tip and replay output. Each invocation returned zero task rows and preserved the event, binding, watermark and authenticated custody snapshot.
- **Confirmed import before caller acknowledgment:** On x64-review (kernel `6.8.0-142-generic`), the receipt harness observed the dedicated proof importer stop after a successful ordinary import returned to the CLI. It killed and reaped that PID before the result print and verified empty stdout. The `before_ack_process_kill` log entry records the receipt hash and full reference returned by the normal importer on exact retry. The `before_ack_replay` entries record production replay after death and after ordinary restart.
- **Import fixtures:** x64-review and hosted Ubuntu 24.04, PR #56 head `be29770`. The harness constructed named durable states directly, compared rows and custody, and checked progress or refusal. It also exercised retained epoch handoffs. [Harness](https://github.com/progentic/Warband/blob/be29770/tests/integration/linux/test_receipt_import.py) · [Ubuntu run](https://github.com/progentic/Warband/actions/runs/44840690524/job/136648016700).
- **Gate kills:** x64-review and hosted Ubuntu 24.04, PR #64 head `ef084ab`. The proof build stopped at confirmed claim, primitive return, or confirmed observation. The harness killed the issuer at the selected stop and checked the primitive witness, rows and authenticated custody head on restart. [Harness](https://github.com/progentic/Warband/blob/ef084ab/tests/integration/linux/test_noop_gate.py) · [Ubuntu run](https://github.com/progentic/Warband/actions/runs/44928437067/job/136919570439)
- **Acceptance race:** x64-review and hosted Ubuntu 24.04, PR #65 head `4c0e36c`. The eligible acceptor was stopped and continued. Its competitor received LockUnavailable and then Recorded on exact retry. SQL probes independently rejected duplicate attempt and transition IDs. [Harness](https://github.com/progentic/Warband/blob/4c0e36c/tests/integration/linux/test_noop_gate.py) · [Ubuntu run](https://github.com/progentic/Warband/actions/runs/44934967736/job/136939492660)
- **Head publication kills:** On x64-review (kernel `6.8.0-142-generic`), four builds stopped in `wb_publish` after temporary write, after temporary sync/close, after rename and after directory sync, respectively. The proof hook armed only on appending the first sequence-1 COMMITTED pledge. For each fresh store the harness observed the systemd custodian PID stopped, killed it by PID descriptor and collected exit 1 / AttestationUncertain from the production importer. Before recovery it independently reconstructed the canonical event, pledge, two history frames and new head, and inspected both head names. The four `publication_kill` entries identify the stop, caller result, pre-recovery files and recovered receipt.
- **Custodian evidence:** x64-review, PR #49 head `67195bf`. A first pre-enrollment REJECTION file sync succeeded and its directory sync was forced to fail. The response had status 14 and a zero receipt. A separate kill after a successful denial preserved the retrievable receipt. A shortened head produced a read-only diagnostic. [Target record](isolated-linux-custodian.md) · [Harness](https://github.com/progentic/Warband/blob/67195bf/tests/integration/linux/test_custody_denial.py)
- **Pure history tests:** supplied bytes cover stale or contradictory heads, invalid links and both classifications of a truncated suffix. These are classification assertions. Disk repair and recovery after a process death require target evidence. [Tests at inventory baseline](https://github.com/progentic/Warband/blob/2f12efc/tests/odin/custody/history_test.odin)

**Storage errors at points 1, 3, 5, 6 and 10.** Exercise short writes, ENOSPC and fsync failures at the applicable write/sync edges, grouping only identical durable outcomes. A completed short write must preserve exact bytes. An unrecoverable storage error requires CHECKPOINT_STORAGE_FAULT, withheld progress and operator reconciliation. Record any zero-receipt fault reply. Record torn or retained bytes after short writes at PENDING/COMMITTED/GENESIS/EPOCH append and head publication. At point 3 inspect the journal after a SQLite write, full-disk or sync failure. Keep the first-create denial sync case as evidence for that specific edge. Complete checksum/link damage or interior corruption requires ATTESTATION_HISTORY_FAULT, never tail repair. No OS-crash or power-loss run is established by these process tests.

**Point 9 call-path check.** Observation and evaluation both call `append_noop` → `append_pledged` → `commit_event` → `confirm_stored`. Acceptance uses `append_acceptance` → `append_pledged`, and also rechecks eligibility and inserts the attempt binding in the transaction. The import kill hook is restricted to receipt imports. ## Separate remaining proofs

- **Replay evidence for remaining runs.** Apply the [qualified replay command](../decisions/0005-event-integrity-acceptance-and-recovery.md#slice-6-functional-replay-qualification) to each newly retained artifact. The functional proof covers the four live gate fixtures and one independent mixed history. Point 4 now also has replay evidence on its own retained state at all three observations.
- **Capacity boundaries.** Run recovery over single ordinary histories of 100 and 10,000 records. Separately count repeated examinations for denial-heavy histories requiring exactly 100,000 or 100,001 visits. At the cap, readiness requires complete successful validation before the time deadline. Needing another visit yields RECOVERY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED on each startup. Record actual visits and budget debits separately. The [segmented Slice 4 samples](slice4-capacity.md) and [Slice 5 task checkpoint](slice5-capacity.md) do not exercise these boundaries.

Keep the pledge and suffix intact and withhold automatic cancellation, re-attestation and redispatch.
