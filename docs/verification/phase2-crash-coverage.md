**Storage errors at points 1, 3, 5, 6 and 10.** Exercise short writes, ENOSPC and fsync failures at the applicable write/sync edges, grouping only identical durable outcomes. A completed short write must preserve exact bytes. An unrecoverable storage error requires CHECKPOINT_STORAGE_FAULT, withheld progress and operator reconciliation. Record any zero-receipt fault reply. Record torn or retained bytes after short writes at PENDING/COMMITTED/GENESIS/EPOCH append and head publication. At point 3 inspect the journal after a SQLite write, full-disk or sync failure. Keep the first-create denial sync case as evidence for that specific edge. Complete checksum/link damage or interior corruption requires ATTESTATION_HISTORY_FAULT, never tail repair. No OS-crash or power-loss run is established by these process tests.

**Point 9 call-path check.** Observation and evaluation both call `append_noop` → `append_pledged` → `commit_event` → `confirm_stored`. Acceptance uses `append_acceptance` → `append_pledged`, and also rechecks eligibility and inserts the attempt binding in the transaction. The import kill hook is restricted to receipt imports. Needing another visit yields RECOVERY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED on each startup. Record actual visits and budget debits separately.
